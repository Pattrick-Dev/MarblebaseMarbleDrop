The Recycler

The Recycler is a station based on a grindstone. You can craft your own (see the new player tutorial for the recipe) and place it anywhere, or use one already placed. Hold the marble you want to recycle, then Shift + Right-click the station. You can also open it with /md recycler.

What Happens

  • You get a Dust refund based on the marble's rarity.
  • The marble is destroyed.
  • This is one-way under normal play.
RarityDust refund
Common5
Uncommon10
Rare20
Epic40
Legendary80

When to Use It

Recycle weak duplicates or failed experiments to recover Dust for your main racer.

Alias command: /md recycle
