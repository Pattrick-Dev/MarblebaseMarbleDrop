The Upgrade Station

The Upgrade Station is a station based on a smithing table. You can craft your own (see the new player tutorial for the recipe) and place it anywhere, or use one already placed. Run /md upgrade or right-click the station to open it.

What It Does

You spend Dust to directly increase one stat of a marble you already own. This is different from infusion, which creates a new marble.

Cost

Each point costs more as that stat gets higher, and higher-rarity marbles cost more per point to upgrade in the first place. Every stat is capped per the rarity table on the Marbles & Rarity page — you cannot upgrade a stat past your marble's rarity cap.

RarityBase cost per point
Common5 Dust
Uncommon8 Dust
Rare12 Dust
Epic18 Dust
Legendary25 Dust

On top of the base cost, every 10 points already in that stat adds +1 Dust to the next point's cost.

Upgrade Strategy

  • Prioritize stats that match your racing style first.
  • Avoid spreading Dust too thin across too many marbles early.
  • Upgrading a stat gets more expensive the higher it already is — it's usually more efficient to spread early points across your build before pushing any single stat near its cap.
Alias command: /md upgrades
