Dust & Tasks

Dust is the main currency on MarbleBaseMC. You spend it to infuse new marbles and upgrade your current marbles.

How to Earn Dust

  • Use /md tasks to view daily and weekly objectives.
  • Complete tasks to receive Dust rewards.
  • Everyone gets the same set of tasks — there's no per-player randomization.
TaskResetsReward
Break 128 blocksDaily50 Dust
Kill 12 mobsDaily60 Dust
Walk 1500 blocksDaily40 Dust
Break 2000 blocksWeekly350 Dust
Catch 30 fishWeekly400 Dust

Check Your Balance

Run /md dust at any time to see your current Dust.
