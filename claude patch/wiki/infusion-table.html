The Infusion Table

The Infusion Table is a station based on a cauldron block. You can craft your own (see the new player tutorial for the recipe) and place it anywhere, or use one already placed. Right-click the station, or run /md table while looking at/near it, to open the interface.

What It Does

  • Set a Dust amount to spend (there's a minimum per infusion).
  • Optionally place a catalyst in the catalyst slot — either a vanilla item (different items are worth different amounts) or one of your own marbles (which biases the roll toward that marble's rarity instead).
  • Dust spent + catalyst value together determine your odds at each rarity.
  • Produces a brand new marble.
You can only infuse a limited number of times per day (staff-configurable, currently 5). The exact rarity odds curve and catalyst item values are intentionally not published in full — treat higher Dust + a better catalyst as generally better odds, not a guarantee.

Practical Advice

Treat infusion as your marble source, then use upgrades to tune that marble into a stronger racer.

Command shortcut: /md infusiontable also opens the same station.
