Racing

  • Tracks are built by staff and each track has an ID.
  • Races happen two ways: an automatic scheduled server race that opens on its own, or a race staff opens manually.
  • Join with /md join (scheduled races) or from a race sign / the /md race menu (staff-opened races).
  • If you are not racing, you can spectate live races.
  • Marble stats affect race performance.
You must complete the new player tutorial at least once before you can enter any real race. This does not affect the tutorial's own practice race, which you can always do mid-tutorial.

Scheduled Server Races

On a regular cycle, the server automatically opens one of its eligible tracks for entries. A boss bar at the top of your screen (visible to everyone online, whether you plan to join or not) counts down the time left to enter, and the server announces the track in chat with reminders as the window closes.

  • Type /md join while holding a marble in your main hand to enter.
  • Type /md leave at any time before the race starts to back out and get your marble back immediately.
  • Type /md race next to check whether a race is open, running, or how long until the next one.
  • As soon as one scheduled race finishes, the next one begins its own cycle shortly after — there is always another one coming.

Your Marble During the Race

Entering takes your marble out of your hand for the duration of the race — this is what stops the same marble being entered into two races at once. It is always returned to you once the race ends, or immediately if you back out with /md leave before it starts, whether you placed first or not.

Not Enough Players?

The race still happens even if the lobby is thin, so the schedule never gets thrown off by a quiet server:

  • Two or more real players joined: a normal race, real Dust for the top finishers — not just 1st place (see below).
  • Exactly one real player joined: AI marbles fill the rest of the field so you're not racing alone — you still get a Dust reward based on where you place among them, just a reduced amount since AI was involved.
  • Nobody joined: the race runs as an AI-only showcase for anyone watching. No Dust is awarded since there was no real racer.

Dust Is Split By Place, Not Winner-Take-All

1st place earns the full reward, but 2nd and 3rd still earn a share of it too, so being fast every single race isn't the only way to keep earning Dust from racing. Placing 4th or lower in a given race doesn't earn Dust from that race.

Every race — scheduled or AI-filled — gets the same "Get ready... 3... 2... 1... GO!" countdown and a live standings update as each racer finishes, same as a normal staff-run race.

Staff-Opened Races

Staff can still open any track manually for a one-off race, independent of the automatic schedule. Join those from a race sign at the track or through the /md race menu the same way you always have.

Boost: Your In-Race Skill

Once a race actually launches, every real racer (scheduled races, staff-opened races, and the tutorial's practice race) is handed a Boost item in their hotbar. Right-click it while watching your race to fire a sustained speed burst at a moment of your choosing — timing it well (a straightaway, overtaking into a corner) matters more than just mashing it the instant you get it.

  • Each use consumes one charge; the stack size in your hotbar shows how many you have left.
  • How many charges you start with depends on your marble's Boost stat — higher Boost means more charges to spend during the race.
  • AI opponents never get a Boost item — this is a player-only skill element.
  • Whatever you don't use disappears with the rest of your race inventory once the race ends; it never lingers in your inventory afterward.

How Stats Feel In Races

Better-built marbles perform more reliably and can handle track conditions more consistently than low-stat marbles.
