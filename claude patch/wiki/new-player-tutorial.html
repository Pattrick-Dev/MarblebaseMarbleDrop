New Player Tutorial

On your first join, you will go through a one-time onboarding sequence that teaches the full core loop from earning Dust to racing.

Flow You Will See

  1. Complete a short combat task (you are given the needed weapon).
  2. Craft each of the three stations (Infusion Table, Upgrade Station, Recycler) at a crafting table — the ingredients for the next recipe are given to you automatically as you go.
  3. Learn how to use the Infusion Table.
  4. Learn how to use the Upgrade Station.
  5. Learn how to use the Recycler.
  6. Finish with a practice race against AI.

Why It Matters

This onboarding gives you enough context to start earning Dust and improving marbles right away, even if you are completely new to the server.

Finishing this tutorial is required before you can join a real race — the practice race at the end doesn't count until you actually complete it. There's no way around this besides playing through it once.
After tutorial completion, open /md tasks and start building your first serious marble.
