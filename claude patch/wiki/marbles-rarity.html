Marbles & Rarity

Every marble has five stats: Speed, Acceleration, Handling, Stability, and Boost. A marble also has a rarity tier that limits how high each stat can go and how high the total stats can go.

Rarity Per-stat cap Total cap
Common60250
Uncommon68280
Rare75310
Epic82340
Legendary90370

Important

Higher rarity means a higher build ceiling. It does not guarantee better starting stats. You still need to earn Dust and upgrade your marble over time.

Tip: Keep one reliable marble for racing and one backup for risky upgrades.
